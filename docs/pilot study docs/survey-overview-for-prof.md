Please find attached the facilitator script and two survey forms for the CoGEN pilot study. Here's a quick overview:

---

**Study format**
3 participants — ~30 min collaborative coding session → individual post-survey (8 min) → group discussion with Disney Method redesign activity (~20 min).

---

**Pre-Survey (~5 min, before session)**

1. Demographics — role, years of experience
2. Prior tool experience — AI coding tools, Live Share, collaborative coding, prior AI PM/coordination use
3. Collaboration preferences — 2 Likert items (interpretive context only, not analysed statistically at n=3)
4. Baseline trust in AI — 3 items adapted from Jian, Bisantz & Drury (2000)
5. Open-ended anchors:
   - "What do you expect an AI project manager to do during a coding session?"
   - "What is your biggest concern about letting an AI divide tasks for your team?"
   These two items are mirrored in the post-survey to enable expectation gap analysis.

---

**Post-Survey (~8–10 min, after coding, before group discussion)**

1. General Usability — full SUS scale (10 items, Brooke 1996)
2. Trust in AI — same 3 Jian et al. items as pre-survey (pre→post comparison)
3. Feature Ratings — 5 features each rated 1–5 + required open-ended explanation (onboarding, AI task division, task tracker, @AI chat, collaboration awareness)
4. AI as Project Manager — directional scale (chatbot ↔ PM) + 3 open-ended questions including expectation gap mirrors
5. Individual Reflection — 3 open-ended questions (most frustrating moment, most helpful thing, unmet needs)

Total: 30 questions.

---

**Questions for your feedback**

1. Should the SUS be included in the pilot, or reserved for the final study (n=15–20) where it carries more statistical weight?
2. We use 3 adapted items from Jian et al. (2000), not the full 12-item scale. Is "adapted from Jian et al." an acceptable framing in the thesis?
3. The two collaboration preference items (pre-survey) are not statistically analysable at n=3. Worth keeping as interpretive context, or remove to keep the form lean?
4. Do you recommend a second coder for qualitative analysis, or is single-coder thematic analysis with member-checking sufficient for a pilot chapter?
